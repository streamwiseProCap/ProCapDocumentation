# Features and editions

ProCap is available in four editions. The edition you have is shown in the title of **Software Settings**, for example **PROFESSIONAL 2027.0**.

| Edition | Intended for |
| --- | --- |
| **Professional** | The full measurement and analysis package |
| **Compact** | Live measurement with essential visualization |
| **Professional Reader** | Analyzing existing measurements without a probe or tracking system (replay only) |
| **Student** | Teaching and training |

## Limits by edition

| Limit | Professional | Compact | Professional Reader | Student |
| --- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Visualization planes | 10 | 5 | 10 | 10 |
| Isosurfaces | 10 | – | 10 | 10 |
| Models | 15 | 5 | 15 | 15 |

{% hint style="info" %}
Buttons in the display header and panels in the hotbar are hidden when your edition does not include the feature.
{% endhint %}
